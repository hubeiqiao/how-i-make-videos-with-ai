# Finished episode to social short
Portable teaching adaptation of the short-form workflow shown in the film, informed by Joe’s ai-candidate-growth-short instructions. Not a one-click clone of the original project.

Inputs: accepted long video, corrected transcript, intended viewer, format and action.
1. Identify a self-contained exchange or transformation with an immediate reason to watch.
2. Preserve context and the complete response; do not invent an outcome in the hook.
3. Open on the useful interaction rather than a long branded introduction.
4. Use readable captions and distinguish active speakers where relevant.
5. Fit the crop to the actual subject and important screen content.
6. Add a brief ending that makes the next action clear.
7. Check voice balance, captions, cuts and the first frame.
8. Prepare a matching cover and concise copy. Inspect the actual upload preview.
9. Approve the source-language edit before making localized variants.

A short is a new edit with its own rhythm, not simply the first two minutes of the long video.
