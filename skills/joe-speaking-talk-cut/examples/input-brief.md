# Input brief (teaching example)

A made-up example that shows the level of detail. It is not one of Joe's projects. Replace every line with your own.

Workflow: joe-speaking-talk-cut
Source file: originals/meetup-talk.mov (a phone at the back of the room, about 12 minutes)
Speaker photos: originals/photos/ (use one with eyes open)
Product screens: originals/app/ (screen recordings of the feature shown on stage)
Keep: the self-introduction, the live demo from the first tap to the result, the closing lines
Drop: the host's introduction, the Q&A, a first attempt that froze
Cover claim: one line the speaker actually said in the talk; do not write a new one
Destinations and covers: YouTube and LinkedIn (16:9), Instagram and TikTok (9:16 cover), RedNote (4:3 cover)
Translated version: a Chinese line under each English caption, for RedNote
Speaker's own post draft: notes/post-draft.md
Must preserve: the speaker's grammar in the captions
Output folder: exports/

Do not leave source filenames as placeholders when requesting an edit. No private recordings, photos or templates are bundled with this Skill.
