# In-app feature video
Portable teaching adaptation of Joe's joe-speaking-feature-in-app-video workflow. Not the original project, its scripts or its brand assets.

Inputs: a screen recording of a real session, its audio if recorded separately, your icon, wordmark and ending card, an instrumental track, your one-line angle.
1. Intake. Make a working copy at a constant frame rate. If the screen recording has no usable audio, sync the separate audio by cross-correlation and confirm one line by eye. Transcribe with word timestamps and fix mishearings.
2. Map the timeline: hook, conversation moments, the feature's proof, ending, with captions and speaker labels marked per section.
3. Cut conversation moments from the question to the end of a complete answer. Skip silence and empty app states.
4. Captions keep one approved style, sit clear of the platform's buttons and never cover the app's key controls. Speaker labels are plain role names, shown only while that person speaks.
5. Proof lives in the app's own screens, not a designed card. Speed up only the loading, with a small notice on the right wait, then play results at normal speed. If a wrong screen appears, cut to the right moment; never cover the UI.
6. For a launch film: a hook in the creator's own words; a few value cuts, each the real phone playing the moment that proves one claim (about 0.9 s each, at least 1.3 s for the payoff; static cards and chips read as empty); a reveal on a kick; the real how-to-get-it flow; dialogue chapters; the result; your standard ending.
7. Find the kicks on a short rendered mix, not the raw track: the render lands about 2 frames later. Put the cuts on them.
8. Before using any track, transcribe its start: files named like music turned out to be speech. Start at the drop. Demos have no music under the conversation; films duck one bed low under dialogue.
9. When one room mic caught two devices, measure each voice and boost the quieter until both sit within about 2 dB.
10. Balance to -14 LUFS without a dynamic normalizer, which pumped and crushed the music after the conversation. Measure true peak on the encoded file: an encoder once pushed a clean peak over 0 dBTP.
11. Check the final file: frame 0, the hook's exit, labels only during speech, the speed-up ending before results, the ending, every highlight crop, and a text scan every 0.5 s for internal names.
12. Copy: one file per video, a section per platform. Chapters follow topics and useful feedback, never production markers. Lead with a score only when the proof shows it.
13. A Chinese version reuses the approved timeline and proof in a separate file. It never overwrites the English.
