# Recording to finished episode
Portable teaching adaptation of Joe’s joe-speaking-video workflow. Not the original project or its dependent templates.

Inputs: original clip, desired orientation, approved brand assets, transcript language, destination.
1. Inspect the source, make a working copy, and record dimensions, frame rate and audio.
2. Generate a draft transcript/captions; have the speaker correct names and wording.
3. Select complete passages. Keep original speed and speech unless a specific change is requested.
4. Decide whether an introduction is useful. Do not add one automatically.
5. Add readable captions, a restrained brand mark, and a matching ending.
6. Balance sound. Export both orientations only if requested, with separately checked framing.
7. Create cover candidates from actual frames, inspect expressions and upload crops. Make a cover for each platform’s crop: RedNote crops a landscape video’s cover to 4:3, so upload a 4:3 cover with a 16:9 video there.
8. Prepare platform copy with one clear next action.
9. Verify and have a person watch the final export; preserve source and accepted version.

A recording of your own talk at an event goes through joe-speaking-talk-cut instead.

This recipe requires the agent to build or adapt a project with your assets. It does not include Joe’s proprietary branding, raw recordings, or machine paths.
